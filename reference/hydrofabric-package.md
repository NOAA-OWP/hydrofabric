# hydrofabric: Build Hydrologic Modeling Fabrics from Reference Networks

A general toolkit for building hydrologic modeling fabrics from a
reference hydrographic network. Flowlines are split, collapsed, and
reconciled against flow direction and flow accumulation rasters;
catchment divides are partitioned to match; the network is aggregated to
a distribution topology; and per-unit results are merged into global
domains with globally unique identifiers. Outputs are checked against
structural invariants and stamped with build provenance for reproducible
reuse. The reference application is the NOAA Office of Water Prediction
National Water Model (NWM) v4 for the NextGen Modeling System, developed
with partial OWP funding.
